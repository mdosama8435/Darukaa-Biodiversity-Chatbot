# Knowledge Sources

This directory contains authentic, peer-reviewed and authoritative scientific source materials for the DARUKAA.EARTH AI Biodiversity Intelligence platform.

## Source Policy & Integrity Safeguards
- **Zero Fabrication**: No synthetic, fake, or AI-generated papers are permitted.
- **Attribution & Provenance**: Every document is accompanied by companion metadata in `knowledge/metadata/` detailing the publishing body, publication year, DOI, license, and official URL.
- **Supported Formats**: `.pdf`, `.md`, `.markdown`, `.txt`.

## Subdirectories
- `fao/`: Official scientific reports and assessments published by the Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations (FAO), the Intergovernmental Technical Panel on Soils (ITPS), and the Global Soil Biodiversity Initiative (GSBI).
  - Current item: `fao_soil_biodiversity_report_2020.md` (Excerpt of FAO 2020 Soil Biodiversity Report, DOI: `10.4060/cb1924en`, License: CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 IGO).
- `ipcc/`: Assessment reports published by the Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change (IPCC).
  - Current item: `ipcc_srccl_land_degradation_2019.md` (Excerpt of IPCC Special Report on Climate Change and Land, Chapter 4: Land Degradation, DOI: `10.1017/9781009157988.006`, Open Access).
- `research/`: Peer-reviewed academic studies published in indexed journals (*Agriculture, Ecosystems & Environment*, etc.).
  - Current item: `torralba_2016_agroforestry_biodiversity.pdf` (Multi-page PDF research excerpt from Torralba et al. 2016 meta-analysis on agroforestry, soil organic carbon accrual, and floral/faunal species richness, DOI: `10.1016/j.agee.2016.06.002`).
